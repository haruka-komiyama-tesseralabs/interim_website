Tessera raises $60M Series A led by a16z

By Anna Choi · May 5, 2026 at 8:00 AM

We're announcing a $60M Series A led by a16z, with participation from our existing seed investors. It's a vote of confidence in a thesis we've held since we started the company: that ERP modernization has been stuck for a decade not because the underlying technology problems are unsolvable, but because nobody had applied agentic AI to the specific, high-stakes work of planning and executing these migrations safely.

The round lets us do three things faster. First, we're growing the engineering team building out the discovery, planning, remediation, and testing agents that make up the core product. Second, we're extending coverage beyond SAP to the other ERP suites enterprises run alongside it. Third, we're investing in the governance and audit tooling that lets regulated industries adopt this with confidence rather than in spite of their compliance requirements.

None of this happens without the customers who took a chance on us early and the design partners who spent hours walking us through the messy reality of their systems. That feedback shaped the product more than any roadmap we could have written in a vacuum.

We're hiring across engineering, applied research, and customer-facing roles as we put this funding to work. If the problem of moving enterprises off decades-old systems without a multi-year fire drill sounds like the kind of thing worth solving, we'd like to talk to you.
