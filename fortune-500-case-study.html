How a Fortune 500 manufacturer cut migration time by 70%

By Priya Nathan · April 9, 2026 at 2:15 PM

A global manufacturer came to us with a familiar problem: their systems integrator had scoped an ECC to S/4HANA migration at just under three years, most of it front-loaded into discovery and code remediation across two decades of customizations spread over a dozen plants.

We started where we always do — with discovery. Tessera's agents mapped the live system, including the custom objects and integrations that hadn't made it into any documentation still being maintained, and produced a migration plan scoped to real dependencies rather than a sampled subset of them. That plan surfaced in the first two weeks of engagement, not the sixth month.

From there, remediation and testing ran against the plan in governed steps, with the customer's own engineers reviewing each one before it moved forward. Business-critical customizations that needed to be preserved were flagged early, rather than being discovered mid-cutover the way they typically are.

The migration finished in just under ten months — a 70% reduction against the original estimate — without a disruption to plant operations along the way, which was the constraint the customer cared about most.

The specifics vary by customer, but the pattern holds: the majority of migration risk and timeline is concentrated in discovery and remediation, and that's exactly the work that's most tractable to compress once it's handled by agents grounded in the real system rather than a scoping team's best estimate.
