Inside the multi-agent engine that plans SAP migrations

By Priya Nathan · July 14, 2026 at 9:32 AM

A move from SAP ECC to S/4HANA has traditionally meant a multi-year program staffed by armies of consultants, tracing dependencies across custom code, interfaces, and business processes by hand. Most of that time isn't spent migrating — it's spent figuring out what's safe to migrate in the first place.

Tessera replaces that manual discovery with a set of specialized agents that work from a shared plan graph. A discovery agent maps the live system landscape — custom objects, integrations, and the data flowing between them. A planning agent sequences the migration into governed steps, each one scoped to a testable unit of work. Remediation and testing agents then execute against that plan, one step at a time.

The steps aren't just a checklist. Each one is versioned, logged, and reviewed before it runs, with explicit rollback boundaries if something doesn't check out. Nothing executes against a production system without a defined approval point, and every action an agent takes is traceable back to the plan step and the evidence that justified it.

The effect is that the scoping work a systems-integrator team would spend months producing — and would still need to revise once they hit the actual code — gets modeled in days, grounded in the real system rather than a sample of it. That plan becomes the backbone the rest of the migration executes against.

We're continuing to extend the planning agent's coverage to more industry-specific SAP modules this quarter, with the goal of getting a defensible migration plan in front of a customer's team in their first week of engagement, not their sixth month.
