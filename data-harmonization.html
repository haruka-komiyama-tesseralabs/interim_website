Data harmonization across SAP, Salesforce, and Workday

By Anna Choi · February 26, 2026 at 1:20 PM

Most large enterprises run parallel systems of record that were never designed to agree with each other. The same customer might exist under three different IDs across SAP, Salesforce, and Workday, each with a slightly different name, address format, or hierarchy. Day to day, that mismatch is mostly invisible — until a migration forces every one of those records to be reconciled into a single target system.

That reconciliation is where a lot of migrations quietly go over budget. It's rarely a clean one-to-one mapping problem; it's a judgment call, repeated thousands of times, about whether two records represent the same real-world entity and which system's version of the truth should win.

Tessera's data harmonization layer approaches this as a matching and mapping problem rather than a manual reconciliation exercise. It uses probabilistic matching to identify likely duplicates and conflicts across systems, and learns its mapping rules over time from the corrections data owners make, rather than requiring someone to author every rule by hand upfront.

Exceptions the system isn't confident about get routed to the right data owner for a decision, with the specific conflict and both candidate values presented side by side, instead of a spreadsheet of thousands of ambiguous rows.

The payoff shows up after go-live, not just during the migration: a target system built on harmonized data produces far fewer of the break-fix tickets that otherwise show up in the first few months, once people start relying on reports that pull from more than one source system.
